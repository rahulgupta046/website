DESCRIPTION:
PyTorch GAN models generating synthetic time-series CO₂ emission data for 10+ global regions, scaling training data from 120K to 500K+ records. Validated synthetic vs. real distributions using KL divergence and Wasserstein distance. FastAPI services enabled configurable dataset access across 15+ ML workflows. Data preprocessing redesigned from 45 min to under 13 min with Pandas/NumPy.

KEY METRICS:
- 120K → 500K+ records generated
- 10+ global regions covered
- 45 min → 13 min preprocessing
- GPU training: ~10h → ~6h
- Validated with KL divergence + Wasserstein distance
